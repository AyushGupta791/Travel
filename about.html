ABOUT WANDERWISE

Making destination discovery simple and inspiring.

WanderWise is a student-created travel recommendation website designed for people who want quick, useful inspiration before planning a trip.

Our website provides recommendations for beautiful beaches, historic temples and interesting countries around the world.

Our mission is to make travel discovery simple, accessible and enjoyable for everyone. We want visitors to discover new destinations and become more curious about the world.

WanderWise focuses on three main areas: relaxation, culture and exploration.

TEAM GUIDANCE

Instructions for WanderWise team members

Team members should follow these guidelines when creating, reviewing and updating travel recommendations on the website.

01

Research destinations

Check destination information carefully before adding a recommendation. Use reliable and relevant sources whenever possible.

02

Keep information accurate

Review destination descriptions regularly and correct outdated or misleading information.

03

Respect local culture

Write about destinations respectfully. Avoid stereotypes and encourage visitors to respect local communities and customs.

04

Maintain website quality

Check spelling, links, images and page layout before publishing any new content to the website.

05

Communicate with the team

Share important updates with other team members and ask for feedback when making significant changes.

06

Review before publishing

Test the website on different screen sizes and make sure all navigation links, images and forms work correctly.
